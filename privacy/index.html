Access tokens are encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard protocols. We retain access tokens only as long as authorized by the user. You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings or by requesting disconnection.

Users may request deletion of their associated data at any time by contacting us at founder@firkaizen.com. Upon verification, all cached tokens and related metadata will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.
